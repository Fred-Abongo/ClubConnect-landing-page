Features

Scan-to-pay

Streamlined and seamless scan-to-pay experience for customers through the implementation of advanced QR code technology with unique process identification pins representing item and venue details in the respective partnered venue.

Efficient Inventory Management

Providing partnered venues with real-time insights into sales and inventory levels through its QR code technology. Enabling venues to accurately track transactions, manage stock levels effectively, and automate re-ordering processes, thereby reducing loss and optimizing operational efficiency.

Automated Sales Tracking

By seamlessly recording transactions through the QR code technology. This automation ensures accurate and real-time tracking of sales within the partnered venues, facilitating efficient accounting, inventory management, and operational decision-making.

Real-time Insights and Analytics

By continuously updating venues with instant data on sales, inventory levels, and customer behavior through its QR code technology, ClubConnect empowers venues to make informed decisions quickly, optimize operations, and enhance customer experiences in real-time.
